Subject-line and push copy stop defaulting to the first idea.

Subject lines are the single largest test category CRM runs, at roughly 17 specs a month, and today there is no reuse of what already won or lost. This tool generates tone-labeled variants and scores them against a rule set mined from real past tests, so the team tests more angles instead of the first one that comes to mind.
